# mace-sonic-boom-enchantment
MC: Mace Sonic Boom Enchantment
With this datapack you can enchant your Mace with the Sonic Boom ability from the Warden.

You just have to do 2 things.

### 1.
Upgrade your Mace with a Netherite Ingot + Upgrade Template:
Your mace is also safe against lava/fire after this upgrade!
![Reinforced Mace](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/c5055976a3aa7071f96594236231fc2a265a87d8.png)

### 2.
Destroy the warden and receive the Sonic Boom enchantment! Just upgrade your Mace on an anvil with the sonic boom enchantment book. (you have to kill the warden by player!)
After that you can rightclick with your mace to fire the sonic boom ability, consumes 10 Levels per use, 10 seconds cooldown!
